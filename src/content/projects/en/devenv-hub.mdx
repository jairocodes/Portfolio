The template system works like a CLI-only "Spring Initializr": each template declares its options (runtime version, whether to include Postgres/Redis, server type) in a `template.yaml`, and `devenv up` either asks for them interactively or accepts them all at once with `--set key=value --yes` for scripted use.

The admin role has no self-promotion endpoint — it bootstraps from an email allowlist (`ADMIN_EMAILS`) that auto-promotes on next login, avoiding an unnecessary privilege-escalation surface.
